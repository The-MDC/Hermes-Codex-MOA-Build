# Skill: Investor Pitch Materials — MADHATs Gambit

## When to Use This Skill
Trigger automatically when task involves: pitch decks, hotsheets, one-pagers, term sheets,
VC outreach emails, investor Q&A, due diligence docs, financial projections, cap tables.

## Required Context Before Executing
1. Read ABOUT-ME/about-me.md — canonical numbers are non-negotiable
2. Check PROJECTS/investors/ for any active pitch versions
3. Check TEMPLATES/pitch/ for approved formats
4. Verify: platform fee = 1.88%, community share = 28.8% (28% in decks), creator share = 40%

## Output Standards

### For Pitch Decks
- 10–12 slides max for cold outreach
- Order: Problem → Solution → Market → Product → Traction → Business Model → Team → Ask
- Each slide: 1 headline, 3 bullets max, 1 visual
- No slide without a number or proof point

### For Hotsheets / One-Pagers
- Single page, two-column layout
- Header: Company name, tagline, raise amount, valuation
- Sections: What We Do, Market, Traction, Ask, Contact
- All numbers in the canonical format

### For VC Outreach Emails
- Subject line: specific and curiosity-driven (no "exciting opportunity")
- Body: 4 sentences max before the ask
- Sentence 1: What we are in one line
- Sentence 2: The market / why now
- Sentence 3: One specific traction point
- Sentence 4: The ask + CTA
- Attach: hotsheet, link to live deck

## Tone & Language for Investor Materials
- Confident, not desperate
- Specific numbers over adjectives
- Comparison anchors: "Like Polymarket, but for pop culture"
- Risk-aware but optimistic — acknowledge risks and explain mitigation
