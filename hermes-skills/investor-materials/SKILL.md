# Investor Materials

Build investor-facing materials that are consistent, credible, and defensible.

## Golden Rule

All investor materials must agree with each other.

Before writing, confirm the source of truth:
- Traction metrics
- Pricing and revenue assumptions
- Raise size and instrument
- Use of funds
- Team bios and titles
- Milestones and timelines

If conflicting numbers appear, stop and resolve before drafting.

## Core Workflow

1. Inventory canonical facts
2. Identify missing assumptions
3. Choose the asset type
4. Draft with explicit logic
5. Cross-check every number against source of truth

## Asset Guidance

### Pitch Deck Flow
1. Company + wedge
2. Problem
3. Solution
4. Product / demo
5. Market
6. Business model
7. Traction
8. Team
9. Competition / differentiation
10. Ask
11. Use of funds / milestones
12. Appendix

### One-Pager / Memo
- State what the company does in one clean sentence
- Show why now
- Include traction and proof points early
- Make the ask precise
- Keep claims easy to verify

### Financial Model
Include:
- Explicit assumptions visible at the top
- Bear / base / bull cases
- Clean revenue logic layer by layer
- Milestone-linked spending
- Sensitivity analysis for load-bearing assumptions

### Accelerator Applications
- Answer the exact question asked — not what you wish they asked
- Prioritize traction, insight, and team advantage
- Avoid puffery
- Internal metrics must match the deck and model exactly

## Red Flags to Avoid
- Unverifiable claims
- Fuzzy market sizing without stated assumptions
- Inconsistent team roles or titles across docs
- Revenue math that does not sum cleanly
- Inflated certainty where assumptions are fragile

## Quality Gate

Before delivering:
- Every number matches current source of truth
- Use of funds and revenue layers sum correctly
- Assumptions are visible, not buried
- Story is clear without hype language
- Final asset is defensible in a partner meeting
